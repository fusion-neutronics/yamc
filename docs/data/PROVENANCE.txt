Docs data

Small inputs for the executed stellarator examples in docs/sources.md.

- `w7x_fourier_zernike.json` -- the W7-X equilibrium shipped with DESC
  (`desc/examples/W7-X_output.h5`, last member of the family, MIT licence),
  as Fourier-Zernike coefficients in cm (`R_lmn`, `Z_lmn` times 100) with the
  exact-zero modes dropped.
- `cth_like_wout.json` -- `nfp`, `xm`, `xn`, `rmnc` and `zmns` (in m, shape
  `(ns, mn_mode)`) from VMEC++'s `examples/data/wout_cth_like_fixed_bdy.nc`
  (MIT licence), the raw arrays of a VMEC wout file.
