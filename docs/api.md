## Source distributions

The spatial, energy and angular distributions live in the `yamc.sources`
submodule, along with the fusion-spectrum, parametric tokamak and stellarator plasma
builders. Usage examples are under [Sources](sources.md).

## Data helpers

Cross-section inspection, dose and attenuation coefficients, element and
reaction names, and the nuclide cache are accessed through `yamc.data`.
See [Nuclear data](nuclear_data.md) for examples and data configuration.
